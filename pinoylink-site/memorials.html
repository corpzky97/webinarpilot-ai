Memorials & Obituaries

A respectful place for verified family, church and funeral-home memorial notices across Hawaiʻi.

Respect and verification

Family-first, never scraped.

PinoyLink does not automatically copy death notices from social media. Every submission is reviewed before publication.

Please submit only information you are authorized to share publicly. Private family contact information is used for verification and is not published by default.

After the service/public memorial period is complete, the notice is archived by default and removed from active display. Permanent deletion is reserved for an explicit family/admin request or privacy, safety, legal, or correction need.
