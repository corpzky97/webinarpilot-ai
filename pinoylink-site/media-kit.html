Editorial standards

Trust comes first.

Sponsors do not control independent newsroom coverage. Paid placements are clearly labeled as Sponsored, Partner, Advertisement, Business Spotlight or another truthful commercial designation.

PinoyLink may decline misleading claims, illegal offers, deceptive financial promises, unsafe products, political persuasion disguised as news, or campaigns that conflict with community safety and editorial standards.
