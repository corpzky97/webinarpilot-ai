Lost & Found

A moderated way to help reunite people with lost belongings across Hawaiʻi while protecting private information.

Safety first

We protect identifying details.

Posts are reviewed before publication. Do not submit passwords, card numbers, full ID numbers, home addresses, access codes or other sensitive information.

High-risk items: passports, IDs, wallets, bank cards, phones and keys should be described only generally. Claimants should prove ownership privately.

Missing-person cases are not handled as ordinary Lost & Found and require separate verification.
